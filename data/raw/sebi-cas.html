Consolidated Account Statement

Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a single/ combined account statement which shows the details of transactions made by an investor during a month across all Mutual Funds and also other securities held in their demat account(s).

In order to get your CAS, you should update your PAN card number with your depository participant /stock broker.
